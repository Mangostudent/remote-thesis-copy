\section{Conclusion, Limitations, and Future Work}

In this chapter, we study the problem of strategic feature disclosure in the regression setting. 
We showed that under certain conditions on the environment, the receiver can achieve better performance when the sender strategically withholds features. 
We establish conditions on the environment for this to occur and demonstrate how to empirically estimate these conditions in both offline and online settings, providing uniform bounds on the estimation error and online regret bounds. Some limitations of this chapter include the restriction to linear hypothesis classes for both decision and learning problems, and the assumption of all-or-nothing feature revelation. A promising direction for future work is extending the analysis to non-linear hypothesis classes and to partial feature-revelation strategies.
