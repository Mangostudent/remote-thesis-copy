### Next Issue: C2-02 — VC necessity changes the problem (P0 - Blocking)

**Locations:** 
* `Chapter_2_Strategic_Classification/Sections/4B_strategically_aware_erm.tex:3`
* `Chapter_2_Strategic_Classification/Sections/A6_proofs_4B.tex:27`
* `Chapter_2_Strategic_Classification/Sections/A5_proofs_4A.tex:141`

#### What the Reviewer Flagged:
In Chapter 2 (Section 4B), you claim an **"if and only if"** result: that strategic learnability is possible *if and only if* a certain class has finite VC dimension.

The reviewer identified a logical gap in the **necessity direction** ($\implies$):
1. Your setup starts from **deterministic / realizable targets** $Y = h(X)$, where $h \in \mathcal{F}$.
2. To prove that infinite VC implies not learnable (necessity), your proof invokes standard **agnostic PAC learnability** lower bounds. But standard agnostic PAC lower bounds construct adversarial joint distributions over $(X, Y)$ where labels are noisy/random. A lower bound under arbitrary joint laws $(X,Y)$ does **not** automatically prove that the problem is unlearnable when labels are deterministic $Y = h(X)$.
3. In strategic classification, the effective classifier is $r \circ s_r$, and sender utility depends on the true function $h$. It is not a fixed, target-independent hypothesis class.

#### Reviewer's Proposed Fix:
* **Option 1 (Easiest & Safest):** Withdraw the claim of "if and only if". Change the theorem to state that finite VC (or uniform convergence of the strategic loss class) is a **sufficient condition** (an "if" statement) for strategic learnability.
* **Option 2:** If you really want "if and only if", supply an explicit lower-bound reduction with the exact realizable quantifiers, showing that if the class has infinite VC, no strategic learner can learn it under $Y = h(X)$.

What are your thoughts on this one? Do you have an explicit realizable necessity proof, or is dropping "only if" (keeping it as a sufficient condition) acceptable to you?
